# Text bank for tests/load_test.py — OSS models, English + Italian, neutral register.
#
# Format:  <class>\t<text>
# Classes: short | medium | long | conversational | italian
#
# WHY THIS BANK EXISTS. What a hardware benchmark measures — RTF, TTFA, throughput, the
# concurrency ceiling, RSS — is a property of the engine and of the silicon, not of the
# weights. So the bank runs on open weights, and must not depend on one checkpoint's
# language or on a quality metric only that checkpoint can be scored against.
#
# WHY MIXED LENGTHS: request batching behaves differently at different sequence
# lengths — a bank of one sentence measures one corner of the space and calls it the
# answer. The classes are the axis, not decoration. Short requests dominate TTFA;
# long ones dominate throughput and are where a slot stays occupied.
#
# WHY BOTH LANGUAGES: prompt construction differs per language (codec token count),
# so a single-language bank can hide a per-language cost. Italian is the second
# language we ship demos in.
#
# ⚠️ Keep the texts STABLE. They are the x-axis of every cross-box comparison: change
# a sentence and yesterday's numbers stop being comparable with today's.
