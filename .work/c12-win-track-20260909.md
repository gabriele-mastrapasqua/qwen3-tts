# C12-WIN — win the preferred streaming gate at C12 on the frozen Turin architecture

**Task.** After the C16 qualification and its handoff close, make the frozen Turin product
architecture (`turin-c8a-32c-vnni-product`: 4x8 one worker per CCX, cap 4, q4 units, DL-2
elastic 4+4 decoder lane, DL-4 RES1_V2, VNNI Talker/CP, BF16 prefill) sustain C12 with the
full professional streaming contract AND STREAM_RTF p95 <= 0.90, including the short and
conversational tails seen in the sustained soak. Not a generic optimization pass, not a
redesign: one changed mechanism at a time against the frozen control.
**Question.** Which single lever removes the sustained C12 STREAM tail without giving back
cadence, TTFA, quality, stability or rejection semantics?
**Known facts (2026-09-09, handoff `turin-vnni-final-handoff-20260909.md`).** C12 waves
STREAM p95 0.82-0.85; 30-min soak pooled 0.912, short 0.959, conversational 0.914,
medium/long/italian <= 0.90; cadence already good (prebuffer p95 262 ms, safe-start 408,
stall@500 0, mandatory contract PASS). CP 21.5-22.9 ms outside decoder overlap vs
35.2-35.6 inside; Talker 32-35 either way; decoder unit 50.9 mean / 53.7 p95 ms; overlap
share 29.8 % at C12 mixed. RES1_V2 won by cutting decoder residency (overlap share), not
the per-iteration CP tax.
**Unknowns.** Where the post-V2 unit's 50.9 ms go; whether decoder work can preferentially
overlap Talker rather than CP; how much of the short-class tail is the per-request ramp
(1,2,4) fixed cost; whether spin/phase width and the old preparation ideas are material
under the new architecture (old negatives on the pre-lane/pre-V2 engine are not
dispositive, but an absent mechanism in the new top-cost table stays closed).

## Execution order (one candidate at a time; frozen product profile = control)

### C12-WIN-1 Fresh post-V2 causal profile (before any runtime change)
Reproduce the frozen C12 control on the on-demand host (same profile, topology,
model/binary provenance, no stale processes, no hidden env). Bounded DIAGNOSTIC (not a
qualification) with phase/operator timing (`QWEN_STAGE_TRACE=1 QWEN_SD_PHASE=1`, `[SDUP]`
operators) answering: (a) where the 50.9/53.7 ms unit goes after V2; (b) top decoder
operators by wall; (c) fixed cost per q4 unit; (d) materialization/copy/quantization/
rendezvous costs; (e) useful kernel vs preparation/glue share; (f) which engine phases
overlap the decoder; (g) CP wall with/without overlap; (h) Talker wall with/without.
Output: a ranked post-V2 cost table. Decision: ONE target that plausibly removes >= 4-6 ms
of residency or materially cuts CP-overlap exposure.

### C12-WIN-2 Phase-aware decoder overlap (new architectural falsifier, very high priority)
Hypothesis: Talker tolerates overlap, CP does not; if decoder units preferentially overlap
Talker, STREAM improves without a faster decoder. No 1/2-frame units (residency/call
overhead already measured). Smallest policy: bounded start delay of a unit, or start
preferentially during Talker, or yield only at coarse operator boundaries before
CP-critical regions; q4 units and lane ownership unchanged. Instrument/simulate first.
Measure residency, overlap share, CP-overlap fraction, CP p95, Talker p95, STREAM p95,
prebuffer, safe-start, stall@250/@500, mailbox wait/overruns. Kill if residency rises,
mailbox backlog appears, cadence regresses or STREAM does not move.

### C12-WIN-3 Short-class fixed cost
Hypothesis: the startup ramp (1,2,4) is disproportionate on 2-3 s clips. Arms: 1,2,4
(control), 1,4, optionally 2,4 only if TTFA stays inside the gate. No q8 steady state.
Measure short + conversational first: TTFA, STREAM, TOTAL, prebuffer, safe-start, max gap,
stall@100/@250/@500. Goal: lower the short-request fixed-cost share without hurting cadence.

### C12-WIN-4 Old preparation ideas only if the new cost map justifies them
Direct ConvT / dwconv / input, strip/range paths, copy elimination: first show the targeted
operator/copy is a significant fraction of the post-V2 unit, then one flag at a time vs
the control; neutral again = closed under the new architecture.

### C12-WIN-5 Cheap spin / phase-width screen (before deeper code)
A. `QWEN_POOL_SPIN` 4096 / 8192 / 16384 / 65536 (control) — the screen and the frozen
profile used different values. B. Talker and CP measured separately at widths 4, 5/6, 8
with the decoder idle and active (one CCX saturates VNNI GEMV at 2-4 threads); possible
asymmetric phase widths. No width promotion without a serving A/B.

### C12-WIN-6 Opportunistic lane batching (optional, after the above)
When two units of one CCX are ready together, does B2 decoder execution cut total
residency without a burst/cadence regression? Decoder stays off the critical path,
bounded mailbox preserved; compare residency of 2 requests vs 2 units, overlap share, CP
penalty; reject on any prebuffer/safe-start/stall increase. Not a return of inline batching.

### C12-WIN-7 Short A/B gate before any soak
Per candidate: correctness/self-test/parity; isolated commit; clean sync; exact frozen
control; feature arm with ONE mechanism; repeated short C12 waves (short, conversational,
mixed); playback-aware metrics. Promotion screen: zero errors/timeouts, no quality or
cadence regression, stall@500 0, prebuffer/safe-start not materially worse, STREAM gain
above run noise; ideally short p95 toward/below 0.90, conversational <= 0.90. No 30-min
soak for a 1-2 % noisy result.

### C12-WIN-8 Qualification of the winner
Full C12 class waves, long+short, Poisson if relevant, overload semantics unchanged,
30-min C12 soak, per-class and per-5-min-window playback breakdown. Target: STREAM p95
<= 0.90 overall and ideally every class, no class tail, prebuffer p95 <= 500 (target
250-300), safe-start <= 1 s (target 400-500), stall@500 0, no errors, no quality
regression. If short stays slightly > 0.90, report the exact boundary; do not move the gate.

### C12-WIN-9 Capacity curve after the win
Re-screen C10/C11/C12/C13/C14/C16 and classify separately: preferred capacity (<= 0.90 +
playback gates), mandatory-qualified capacity, overload/hard-capacity boundary.

## Stop conditions

old-path retests neutral; spin/width neutral; no repeatable > noise C12 gain → stop stacking
micro-optimizations and hand the evidence to the post-P2/post-Turin architecture review.
