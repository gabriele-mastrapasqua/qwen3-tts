# C12-WIN steps 0–3 — Turin VNNI discriminators

Date: 2026-09-09
Host: AWS c8a.8xlarge, AMD EPYC 9R45, 32 physical cores, one NUMA node, SMT not
supported.
Binary: `e1b1ec7` / SHA256 prefix `63316641bfee2712`; runtime source was clean
`e1b1ec7` for the binary.  The later WAVE runs used a diagnostic-only local
`serving_profile.py` overlay from `e8a9080` to honor the profile's already-declared
`tunable_flags`; no C/runtime object changed.
Profile: `turin-c8a-32c-vnni-product`, 4x8, cap 4, q4, DL-2 elastic decoder lane,
DL-4 `RES1_V2`, per-item INT8 VNNI decoder, native BF16 prefill, synchronous output,
fail-fast queue.  Receive coalescing was 0% in every KPI arm.

## Question

Separate the remaining C12 loss into inline admission/prefill, decoder-overlap/CP
interference, and pool-spin effects before implementing a new mechanism.

The bounded C12 sanity wave was inside the established range: pooled STREAM_RTF
p50/p95 `0.772/0.841`, TTFA p50/p95 `242/272 ms`, required prebuffer p95 `256 ms`,
safe-play-start p95 `469 ms`, stall@250/@500 `0`, errors/rejects `0`.  The 10-minute
control soak had 664 sustained responses, 0 errors/rejects/timeouts; excluding probes,
pooled STREAM_RTF was `0.854/0.923` p50/p95, TTFA `140/172 ms`, prebuffer p95
`258 ms`, safe-start p95 `417 ms`, stall@250/@500 `0`.

## Step 2 / fixed B3 overlap diagnostic

Diagnostic only: `1x8@0-7`, cap 3, five synchronized long waves, `QWEN_COST_MAP=1`
and `QWEN_STAGE_TRACE=1`.  This is not a KPI result.  The elastic lane reserved
step CPUs 0–3 and decoder CPUs 4–7.

From 1,349 `active=3` stage iterations:

| state | iteration share | wall share | CP p50/p95 | Talker p50/p95 | stage wall p50/p95 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| decoder not overlapping | 50.0% | 45.1% | 22.2 / 22.7 ms | 38.1 / 41.1 ms | 60.5 / 63.7 ms |
| decoder overlapping | 50.0% | 54.9% | 36.5 / 37.6 ms | 33.5 / 40.7 ms | 69.7 / 78.2 ms |

The overlap CP tax is therefore about `+14.3 ms` at the median and `+14.9 ms` at
p95.  Talker does not inflate at p95.  The stage trace reports only the elastic
critical-path `decode_ms` (about `0.03–0.04 ms`) because the decoder is external;
it is not the decoder unit wall.

The cost map independently recorded 1,475 decoder calls, `72.5 s` total decoder
wall (`49.2 ms/unit`), `63.8 s` in `decoder.conv_stack`, and `7.6 s` in the
transformer region.  It recorded 35,352 pool dispatches and 465,610 decoder-panel
tasks, but no pool-wait wall duration.  The requested instrumentation does not
bucket CP subregions by overlap.  Aggregate `cp.batch.*` section totals exist
(`gate_up 17.4 s`, `qkv 11.5 s`, `down 7.9 s`, `out_proj 5.5 s`, other 7.0 s),
but overlap-conditioned section attribution is **UNKNOWN**.

The measured overlap share is `54.9%` by active-B3 wall, consistent with the review's
55–60% hypothesis.  Broad CP inflation is supported; the exact cache/bandwidth
mechanism is not yet proven.  Phase-aware scheduling is not justified by this result.

Raw evidence: `.work/evidence/c12win-20260909/b3_diag_1545/`.

## Step 3 / pool spin screen

Three sequential 4x8/C12, three-wave screens; only `QWEN_POOL_SPIN` changed.  All
arms had 36 valid responses, 0 errors/rejects, 0% receive coalescing, and zero
stall@250/@500.

| spin | STREAM p50/p95 | short p95 | conversational p95 | TTFA p95 | prebuffer p95 | safe-start p95 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4096 | 0.781 / 0.849 | 0.852 | 0.816 | 276 ms | 271 ms | 478 ms |
| 16384 | 0.792 / 0.838 | 0.843 | 0.802 | 274 ms | 262 ms | 482 ms |
| 65536 (control) | 0.795 / 0.831 | 0.838 | 0.808 | 273 ms | 261 ms | 474 ms |

Differences are small and not a causal serving win for lower spin.  Keep the frozen
`65536` value; close spin as a tuning path.

## Decision

The active C12 gap is not explained by the helper, and spin is not the lever.  The
highest-information next implementation is one bounded **pre-upsample f32 → BF16
matmat** block, first measured locally/microbenchmarked and then tested for both:

1. decoder-unit residency reduction; and
2. reduction of the measured CP-in-overlap tax.

Do not change scheduler, topology, q4, admission, decoder lane ownership, or add
phase-aware scheduling before that falsifier.  ConvT consolidation and glue removal
remain later only if this first block moves the bounded screen.

cache/bandwidth explanation for CP inflation remains a HYPOTHESIS.
